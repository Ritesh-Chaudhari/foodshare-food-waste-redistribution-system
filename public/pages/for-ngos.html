Find and collect surplus food for those in need

How NGOs Can Participate

Registered NGOs operating within the city can use FoodShare to find and collect surplus food from local donors.

  1. Register Your NGO Create an account with your NGO details and registration information. An administrator will verify your organization.
  2. Browse Available Donations Once approved, browse food donations posted by verified donors — canteens, messes, hotels, and restaurants.
  3. Request a Donation Request the entire quantity of a donation you can collect. The donor will review your request.
  4. Collect the Food After the donor confirms your request, arrange physical collection from the specified location.
  5. Mark as Collected After collecting the food, mark the donation as collected on the platform. The record is saved for all parties.
