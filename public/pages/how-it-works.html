How It Works

A simple step-by-step process for food donation coordination

  1. Donor Registers A food donor (canteen, mess, hotel, or restaurant) creates an account on the platform.
  2. Admin Verifies Donor The administrator reviews and approves the donor account before they can create donations.
  3. NGO Registers An NGO creates an account with their registration details and waits for admin verification.
  4. Admin Verifies NGO The administrator reviews and approves the NGO account before they can request donations.
  5. Donor Posts Surplus Food The approved donor creates a donation listing with food description, quantity, pickup location, and deadline.
  6. NGO Browses & Requests Approved NGOs browse available donations and request the full quantity of a donation they can collect.
  7. Donor Confirms an NGO The donor reviews all NGO requests and confirms one. Other pending requests are closed automatically.
  8. NGO Collects Food The confirmed NGO arranges physical collection from the donor's specified pickup location.
  9. Donation Completed The NGO marks the donation as collected. The record is saved in the system for all parties to see.
