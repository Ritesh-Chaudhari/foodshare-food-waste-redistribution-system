For Food Donors

Reduce food waste by sharing surplus food with your community

Who Can Be a Donor?

Any food establishment with suitable surplus food can register as a donor:

  • College and hostel messes
  • College canteens
  • Hotels
  • Restaurants

How It Works for Donors

  1. Register Your Organization Create an account with your organization details. An administrator will verify your account before you can create donations.
  2. Post Surplus Food When you have surplus food, create a donation listing with food description, quantity, pickup location, and deadline.
  3. Review NGO Requests Registered NGOs will request your donation. Review their details and select one NGO to confirm.
  4. Hand Over Food Once confirmed, the NGO will arrange physical collection. Hand over the food at your specified location.
