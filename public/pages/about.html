About FoodShare

A coordination platform for surplus food redistribution

The Problem

Large quantities of suitable surplus food go unused in messes, canteens, hotels, and restaurants every day. This food is perfectly edible but is discarded simply because there is no organized way to redirect it to those who need it.

At the same time, many registered NGOs are willing to collect and redistribute surplus food, but they lack a simple coordination mechanism to know when and where food is available.

Our Solution

FoodShare bridges this gap by providing a platform where food donors can post surplus food listings and registered NGOs can browse, request, and collect that food. The system tracks the entire process from availability to collection.

🍽️

For Food Donors

Canteens, messes, hotels, and restaurants can easily post surplus food details for NGOs to pick up — reducing waste and helping the community.

🤲

For NGOs

Registered NGOs can browse available donations, view details, and request food. The system coordinates collection between donors and NGOs.

✓

Verified & Managed

An administrator verifies donors and NGOs to ensure reliable coordination. All donations are tracked with clear status updates.

Purpose

This system aims to reduce avoidable food wastage, simplify coordination between food establishments and NGOs, and maintain an organized record of surplus-food redistribution within a single city.
