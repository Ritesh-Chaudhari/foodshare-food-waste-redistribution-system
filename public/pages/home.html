The Problem

Every day, large quantities of perfectly suitable food remain unused in messes, canteens, hotels, and restaurants — especially after regular meal periods. This food is safe to eat but gets discarded simply because there is no organized way to redirect it to people who need it.

At the same time, registered NGOs willing to collect and distribute surplus food lack a simple coordination mechanism to know when and where food is available.

How FoodShare Works

FoodShare acts as a coordination platform between food donors and verified NGOs within a single city. The process is simple:

Donor
Lists surplus food
→
NGO
Browses & requests
→
Donor
Confirms NGO
→
NGO
Collects food

Each donation goes through a clear workflow: the donor creates a listing, NGOs request it, the donor confirms one NGO, and the NGO physically collects the food. Every step is tracked in the system.
